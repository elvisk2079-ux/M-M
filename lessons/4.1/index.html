7. Read: The First Snow Day

Read the short story and choose the answer. No audio in the story.

It is morning. Matvey and Makar see the first snow. White snowflakes fall from the clouds. Spot the dog runs outside.

The boys see a frozen puddle. “It is ice now!” says Matvey. “Water can freeze when it is very cold,” says Mum. There are long icicles on the roof.

At noon the sun comes out. The ice gets warm and begins to melt. It changes into water again. The boys make a little snowman. They love winter!
