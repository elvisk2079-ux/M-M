6New science story — The Bird Bowl

This is a new text, not the story from the lesson. Read or listen and answer.

On Sunday, Makar looks into the garden. There is a small bowl of water for the birds. In the morning, the bowl has ice in it. The night was very cold.

Makar calls Matvey. “Look! The water is frozen!” he says. Matvey touches the ice. “It is hard and cold. The birds cannot drink it,” he says.

The boys take the bowl into the house. It gets warm, and the ice begins to melt. Soon there is water in the bowl again. The boys put fresh water outside for the birds. “We are winter scientists!” says Makar.
