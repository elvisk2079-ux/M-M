7. Read: A Snowy Saturday

Read the story and choose the answer. No audio in the story.

It is Saturday morning. Matvey and Makar look out of the window. The sky is blue and the snow is white. ‘Let’s go outside!’ says Matvey. The boys put on warm coats, hats and gloves. Their dog Spot is very excited.

First, the boys make a big snowman in the garden. Makar finds a carrot for its nose. Then they make snowballs and have a friendly snowball fight. Spot runs after the snowballs, but he cannot catch them all!

After lunch, the boys go sledding in the park. Matvey loves going fast, but Makar likes going slowly. They see some children skiing and ice skating. ‘I want to try skiing one day,’ says Makar.

In the afternoon, the wind gets stronger. It is cold and snowy. The boys go home and drink hot chocolate. ‘I love winter because we can do so many fun things!’ says Matvey. ‘Me too!’ says Makar.
